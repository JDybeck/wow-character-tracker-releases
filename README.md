# WoW Character Tracker

Posts your World of Warcraft moments to a Discord channel the moment they happen: level-ups, end boss kills (with your group), rare, epic and legendary loot, new mounts, max level and hardcore deaths, optionally with an in-game screenshot. The app also shows all your characters with their level, item level, gold, professions, highlights and level history.

Made with WoW Forever (Classic+) in mind, and works for Retail too.

## Download

Get **WoWCharacterTracker.exe** from the [latest release](https://github.com/JDybeck/wow-character-tracker-releases/releases/latest). Nothing else to install. Windows might show a SmartScreen warning since the exe isn't signed: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

Start it once and it sets everything up: it finds your game, installs the addon, and walks you through adding a Discord webhook. After that it waits in the tray and opens when you start WoW. It also checks here for updates and can update itself.
